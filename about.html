About the College

Excellence in Technical Education

Our Legacy

Established by EASA Foundations, Coimbatore, EASA College of Engineering and Technology is dedicated to providing high-quality instruction and training in the fields of engineering and technology.

Our institution is a Sourastrian minority institution with a futuristic and global outlook, preparing students for the challenges of the modern professional world.

Our Vision

To be a premier institution that creates innovative leaders for the global society.

Our Mission

To impart quality education and foster research and development in all spheres of learning.

Core Values

Excellence, Integrity, Innovation, and Social Responsibility in all our endeavors.
