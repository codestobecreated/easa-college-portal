Course Overview

The B.E. in Cyber Security at EASA College is designed to meet the growing global demand for security professionals. Our curriculum blends theoretical foundations with intense practical training in our state-of-the-art Security Operations Center (SOC) lab.

Students learn to identify vulnerabilities, perform ethical hacking, manage risk, and implement robust security measures across diverse digital landscapes. We prepare our graduates to be the first line of defense in an increasingly connected world.
