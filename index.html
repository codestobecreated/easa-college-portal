Best Engineering College in Coimbatore

The College was established by EASA Foundations, Coimbatore, a public charitable and educational trust, under the Chairmanship of its Managing Trustee, Thiru.T.D.Eswaramoorthy. The College is a Sourastrian minority institution.

Our objective is to impart high-quality instruction and training and foster research in the fields of science, engineering, technology and management by establishing educational institutions with a futuristic and global outlook.

Well connected by air, rail and road to all major cities in India, it offers many advantages in terms of its strong science and technology base and abundant educational resources.
